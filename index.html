Before you decide

  • Import VAT cancels most of the saving. Once you pay the 20% VAT at UK customs, a US purchase only saves you about £20–£140. The saving is largest in a state with no sales tax, like Oregon (also Delaware, Montana and New Hampshire).
  • US models are eSIM-only and have no physical SIM tray. Make sure your UK network supports eSIM. EE, O2, Vodafone, Three and most MVNOs do.
  • Warranty and repairs: AppleCare and some repair services can be limited to the country where you bought the phone. A UK purchase also gets UK Consumer Rights Act protection.
  • No tourist tax refund in CA or MI. Texas has a tax-free shopping scheme for international visitors, but only at retailers that take part. Check before you rely on it. Apple Stores usually don’t.
  • Card fees matter: a 2.99% foreign-transaction fee adds roughly £35. Pay in USD, not GBP. If the terminal offers to charge you in pounds (“dynamic currency conversion”), the rate is usually worse.
  • All figures are estimates. They aren’t tax or financial advice.
